================================================================================
HOW TO USE
================================================================================

FOR VENUE MANAGERS:
1. Go to Venues Management → Add/Edit Venue
2. Navigate to Step 3: Pricing & Media
3. Find "Peak Hours Configuration" section
4. Enable peak hours and set:
   - Start time (e.g., 17:00 for 5 PM)
   - End time (e.g., 23:00 for 11 PM)
   - Price multiplier (e.g., 1.5 for 50% markup)
5. Save the venue

EXAMPLE:
Base Price: PKR 2,000
Peak Hours: 5:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Multiplier: 1.5
Result: Peak Price = PKR 3,000 (+50%)

================================================================================
REVENUE IMPACT
================================================================================

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
- 10 bookings per day
